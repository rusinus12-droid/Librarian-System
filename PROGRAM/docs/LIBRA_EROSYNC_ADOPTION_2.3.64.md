## Adopted in the Librarian's fixed prompt

1. Mention, recollection, dreams and plans cannot establish physical presence. Preserve departures and scene transitions; returning requires source evidence.
2. A proposed or ignored instruction cannot become a completed event. Record only an executed portion as an event and keep intentions/unresolved goals distinct from observed outcomes.
3. Temporary reactions/current conditions cannot alone establish permanent traits or preferences. One response following a suggested script is not lasting development. Preserve relationship direction/context instead of generalizing one pair to everyone.
4. Do not blend incompatible alternate-world histories or personalities into one current record. This reinforces existing worldline/source boundaries rather than introducing a new cross-world merge mechanism.
